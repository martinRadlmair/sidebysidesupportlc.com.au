# Our Services

At Side By Side Support, we provide person-centred support services for NDIS participants across Mount Gambier, Millicent, Naracoorte, Penola and the wider Limestone Coast region of South Australia.

Our focus is on helping participants build capacity, increase independence, connect with services, and work towards the goals outlined in their NDIS plan. We work alongside participants, families, carers, service providers and community organisations to ensure supports are coordinated and effective.

# Support Coordination

Support Coordination helps participants understand and implement their NDIS plan. We assist participants to identify suitable providers, coordinate supports, build their confidence in managing services, and make informed decisions about their future.

Our goal is to help participants develop the skills and knowledge needed to navigate the NDIS while increasing their independence and choice over time.

# Specialist Support Coordination

Specialist Support Coordination is available for participants with more complex support needs who may face significant barriers to achieving their goals.

We work collaboratively with participants, families, allied health professionals, service providers and government agencies to address challenges, reduce risks, and establish sustainable support networks. This may include support during periods of crisis, major life transitions, housing challenges, or involvement with multiple service systems.

# Community Case Management

Community Case Management provides practical assistance to help individuals access and coordinate services across disability, health, mental health, housing, education, employment and community sectors.

We advocate for participants, facilitate communication between services, and help ensure supports work together effectively to meet individual needs and goals.


# Who We Support

We have experience supporting people with a range of disabilities and support needs, including psychosocial disability, autism, intellectual disability, complex support needs, and young people transitioning into adulthood.

If you would like to learn more about our services or discuss how we can support you or your family, please contact us for an obligation-free conversation.
